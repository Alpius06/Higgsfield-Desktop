Where images and videos you download from Higgsfield end up.

Download folder Downloads are saved here. Files from a Cinema Studio project go into a subfolder named after the project.

Projects

Every project you open in Cinema Studio is listed here and in the toolbar's Projects ▾ menu. Pin one to give it its own toolbar button. Files you download from a tab inside a project are saved in that project's own folder.
